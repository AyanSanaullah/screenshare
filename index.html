Turn on Face stickers and make the face under a sticker to send it. Face tracking runs on your own computer, so only the sticker goes to them.

        

      

    


    

      
To share a movie, open it in another Chrome or Edge tab, press Share a tab, pick that tab, and keep “Also share tab audio” on. Whoever shares should wear headphones.

      
Netflix and most paid services show up black when shared. For those, play it on both laptops with Teleparty and use Pop out to keep each other on screen.

      
Stickers pop up next to whoever sent them, on both screens. Face stickers are a per-person switch, so each of you turns it on for your own face. The first time, it takes a few seconds to download the face model (about 10 MB), then your browser keeps it.
